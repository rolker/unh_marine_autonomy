## Statements written today that the history contradicts
- "Rebuilt tile: all pings" — the 2026-09-02 rebuild dropped 58,501 of 21.0 M pings (no transform at bag starts) and 48,558
  lacked heave. A rebuild is as complete as the recording allows; the build record should hold the counts.
- "Live tiles are replaced by rebuilt tiles" — ADR-0010 D8 clears live cells only where the rebuild has data.
- "Today's data distinct from earlier days'" — per-day partitioning was built (uma#147) and removed (uma#221): a calendar day
  is a weak proxy for a survey (midnight UTC splits an evening session). The goal needs another basis.
- "Nothing assumes RTK GPS" vs the vertical world being GNSS-ellipsoidal with water level measured from GNSS (ADR-0002 D4,
  ADR-0010 D5).
